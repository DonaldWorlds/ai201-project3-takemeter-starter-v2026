# My label definitions, one per line, as `label: definition`.
# Copied from the Label Taxonomy section of README.md. baseline.py reads this
# file and uses these sentences — not the label names — as the prompt.

analysis: a post that gives a reason for its claim, a why or a comparison or a piece of history that the claim rests on
hot_take: a claim or answer about the music or the artists stated flat, with no reasoning attached, even when it is factual
reaction: the writer responding to the thing itself rather than claiming anything about it, surprise or amusement or a memory of encountering it
